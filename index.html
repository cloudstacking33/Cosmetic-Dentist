A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone. Over a few months it fuses with the bone and becomes a stable anchor for a crown, bridge or denture. Because the implant replaces the root as well as the visible tooth, it protects the jawbone and keeps neighboring teeth in place. This is why dental implants are widely considered the gold standard for tooth replacement.

Dental Implant Options for Every Situation

No two smiles are the same, so we offer a full range of implant treatments. A single missing tooth can be replaced with one implant and a custom crown. Several missing teeth can be restored with an implant supported bridge. When most or all teeth are missing, full mouth dental implants and All On 4 dental implants can rebuild an entire arch using as few as four implants.

Patients who have worn traditional dentures for years often tell us that loose fit, sore gums and food restrictions are the biggest frustrations. Implant supported dentures solve these problems by locking the denture onto implants, which gives you far more stability and bite strength. Many patients choose this option when they want a more confident, comfortable daily experience.

Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants

Compared with removable options, implants offer a stronger bite, clearer speech and a more natural feel. They stop the bone loss that follows tooth loss, which helps keep your face shape youthful. They also protect neighboring teeth, because no healthy tooth needs to be ground down to support a bridge. For many patients, the biggest benefit is simply forgetting that a tooth was ever missing.

Because implants are cleaned with regular brushing and flossing, daily care is simple. Routine visits let us check the gums and the restoration so small issues are handled early.

Common Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental implants can last for decades. The crown on top may need replacement after many years of wear, but the implant itself is designed for long term use.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Treatment is done with local anesthesia, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. Mild soreness afterward is normal and is usually managed with simple medication.

What is All On 4 and who is it for?

All On 4 uses four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth. It is often suited to patients who are missing most or all of their teeth and want a fixed solution.

How long does the full process take?

Timelines vary. Healing and bone fusion commonly take a few months, and your plan will show the expected schedule for your case.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with enough jawbone and healthy gums are candidates. If bone is limited, additional steps may help. A consultation will confirm your options.
